History · 2020

A careful ceremony, held in the middle of a pandemic.

The 2020 Vishnupuram Literary Circle Award was presented to the writer Suresh Kumar Indrajit at a small, quiet gathering in Madurai — translated and adapted from the original Tamil account.

A plan changed by COVID-19

The Circle had initially decided against holding a ceremony that year because of the pandemic — as had been done for the Kumarakuruparan Award, the plan was for members already living in the laureate's city to visit him quietly and offer their congratulations. But there was no Vishnupuram Literary Circle presence in Madurai itself, so a small group decided to travel there in person to present the award.

Suresh Kumar Indrajit had been undergoing treatment for several long-standing health conditions, and his family was taking careful precautions around his health. Because of this, he could not meet many friends at once — only a very small number of visitors were permitted.

The recipient

Suresh Kumar Indrajit received the Vishnupuram Literary Circle Award for 2020, in recognition of his contribution to Tamil literature and literary criticism.

The ceremony

The gathering took place over 24–25 December 2020 in Madurai, at the Royal Servicestat Apartments, with friends travelling in from Coimbatore, Erode and other cities to keep the group small. Among those present were Jeyamohan, Lakshmi Manivannan, Sushil Kumar, K. P. Vinodh, Krishna (Erode), Ram Kumar, Cuius Senthil, Narendran, Dr. Ravi, GSSV Naveen, Ilangowan Muththaiah and Athmaarthi, along with Suresh Kumar's wife and her two siblings. District judge Prem Anand helped arrange a temple visit for the group.

The ceremony itself began at 2:30 in the afternoon. Krishna, of the Erode chapter, opened with an introduction to the Vishnupuram Literary Circle. Cuius Senthil then read out the award citation. K. P. Vinodh's short documentary about Suresh Kumar, Tharcheyalgalin Varaipadam (“A Map of Accidents”), was screened for the gathering. Ram Kumar presented the award trophy, Sunil Krishnan spoke on his research into Suresh Kumar's work, and Jeyamohan closed the formal proceedings with a few remarks. A book was released at the ceremony: Suresh Kumar Indrajit — Valarum Vaasippu (“A Growing Practice of Reading”).

The formal ceremony lasted a little over two hours, but the literary conversation among friends continued well past it, running until eleven that night.
