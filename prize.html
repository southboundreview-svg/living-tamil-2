The Prize

The Living Tamil Award for World Literature.

An annual international honour, initiated by the Living Tamil Association for World Literature (LTAWL) in 2026, given to a living writer whose life of work has enlarged the possibilities of literature.

What it is

The Living Tamil Award for World Literature is the largest literary award ever to originate from India. It is presented each year to a living writer whose body of work — across fiction, poetry, essay, drama, memoir or criticism — has shaped the literature of a language and reached readers beyond it.

The award is administered by the Living Tamil Association for World Literature (LTAWL): a working readership of thousands, across four continents, who read and discuss serious literature every month.

Eligibility

  • The writer must be living at the time of nomination.
  • The body of work must span at least twenty-five years of published writing.
  • Any literary language, any country of residence, any citizenship.
  • Self-nomination is not accepted.

Nomination

Nominations are invited from universities, national and international literary societies, publishers of literary work, past laureates, and members of the Living Tamil Committee. A single nominator may submit one candidate per cycle.

The nomination dossier should include a critical statement, a list of major works with translations, and two supporting letters. Full guidelines are sent to nominating institutions each May.

The annual cycle

  • May — call for nominations opens.
  • October — nominations close.
  • February — the jury announces the shortlist.
  • April — the laureate is named at the Living Tamil LitFest.

The award

The laureate receives a citation, a medal, and a purse. The Prize also underwrites a year-long programme of translations and public readings so that the laureate's work reaches new audiences in new languages — carried across LTAWL's chapters in India, the United States, the United Kingdom and Europe.

A programme, not just a prize

The Award sits inside a larger literary programme: the annual Living Tamil LitFest — first held in New York on 3–4 April 2026 with more than fifty writers and 300 attendees — and the long-running awards of LTAWL for Tamil writers, given every year since 2010.
