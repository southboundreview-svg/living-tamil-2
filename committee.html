Committee & Coordinators

A working readership on four continents.

The Living Tamil Association for World Literature (LTAWL) is a network of readers, writers and translators active since 2010. The Award is guided by a founding voice, an operations team, a booklist committee that reads and reviews, and a rotating international jury.

Founder & Guiding Voice

Jeyamohan
Founder · Writer

Tamil novelist and essayist whose readership founded LTAWL in 2010. His 2025 US tour for Stories of the True (Farrar, Straus and Giroux) sparked the Living Tamil programme.
