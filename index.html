Living Tamil Association for World Literature (LTAWL) · Est. 2026

The world's largest literary award to originate from India.

The Living Tamil Award for World Literature honours a living writer whose lifetime of work, in any language, has enlarged the possibilities of literature. Presented annually by a global network of readers, writers and translators.
