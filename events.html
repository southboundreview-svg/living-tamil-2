The Living Tamil Literature Festival.

An annual global gathering — Bringing Modern Tamil Literature to the World. Coordinated by the Living Tamil Association for World Literature (LTAWL). The Living Tamil Award for World Literature is announced each year at the festival's closing ceremony.

2026 · Concluded

3 – 4 April 2026 · New York

New York Living Tamil Literary Festival 2026.

The inaugural edition, held at the New York Academy of Medicine. Two halls, sixteen sessions across two days, roughly 300 attendees. Thirty Tamil writers from India, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, Malaysia and Singapore joined thirteen American writers as guests. Sessions were coordinated by the younger generation born and raised in America.

Ambassadors: Kamal Haasan & Mani Ratnam. Lead coordinators: V. Soundararajan, Maheshwari Ramar, Rajan Somasundaram. Resource persons: A. Muttulingam & Jeyamohan. External coordinator: Arun Mahizhnan. Sponsor: Takshashila University.

Delhi Living Tamil Literary Festival 2028.

The third edition — and the first held on Indian soil. Delhi hosts a gathering that brings modern Tamil literature into conversation with India's other literary languages and the international readership the festival has been building since 2026.
